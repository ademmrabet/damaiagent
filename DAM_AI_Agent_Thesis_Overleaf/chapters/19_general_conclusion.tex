\markboth{General Conclusion}{General Conclusion}

This thesis delivers a working, deployed, tested AI agent that answers questions about a real, complex compliance document with verified factual accuracy, built through a disciplined, evidence-driven engineering process from raw PDF extraction through to a hosted multilingual chat application. Every phase of the CRISP-DM cycle is represented not as a formality but as real, substantial engineering work: Data Understanding and Data Preparation in particular consumed the largest share of total effort, which is itself an honest and instructive finding about where the real difficulty lies in building a trustworthy AI system over a messy, real-world source document -- not in the LLM integration, which by comparison was the more straightforward half of the project once the underlying data and retrieval layer could be fully trusted.

Natural next steps, in rough priority order based on where the project's own decision log flags the largest remaining gaps: resolving the two remaining \texttt{threshold\_variant} geometry limitations with true character-level superscript detection; independently verifying the LLM-dependent fixes made in September 2026 against a real, unblocked Groq API connection; having the five-language interface translations reviewed by native speakers; and, longer-term, revisiting whether the accumulated real-query corpus (now large enough, after months of live testing, to plausibly constitute a small labeled dataset in its own right) changes the earlier conclusion that there was not enough data to justify a custom-trained retrieval model.

Beyond its immediate technical deliverable, this project is offered as a case study in a broader argument: that a large language model can be safely deployed against a compliance-sensitive institutional document, without accepting the hallucination risk normally associated with generative AI, provided the model is architecturally restricted to rephrasing already-verified facts rather than ever being trusted to retrieve or decide what those facts are. Every design decision in this thesis that mattered -- the knowledge graph, the grounding check, the deterministic fallback -- follows directly from taking that constraint seriously from the very first day of the project rather than treating it as an afterthought to be bolted on once a first version was already generating plausible-sounding answers.
