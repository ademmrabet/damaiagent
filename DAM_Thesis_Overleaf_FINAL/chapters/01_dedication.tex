\noindent I dedicate this work:

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent\textbf{To my father,}\\
who taught me what quiet determination looks like long before I understood the word. You believed in me on the days I couldn't manage it myself, and every value I brought to this work started with something you showed me first.

\vspace{0.4cm}
\noindent\textbf{To my mother,}\\
for a love that never once wavered, for sacrifices whose size I only understood years later, and for being the calm I could always come back to, no matter how heavy this project got.

\vspace{0.4cm}
\noindent\textbf{To my brother,}\\
who has been a steady presence my whole life, and who never had to be asked.

\vspace{0.4cm}
\noindent\textbf{To my girlfriend,}\\
thank you doesn't cover it. You were patient on the nights this project took more than I had planned to give. You believed in me when I doubted myself, and you stood beside me through the hardest stretches without ever making me feel like a burden. Your support carried more of this than you know.

\vspace{0.4cm}
\noindent\textbf{To my friends,}\\
Ayoub and Alaa, whom I met during this Master's and could not be more grateful to have found. To Fayrouz and Houde, for always being there. And to Brahim, my childhood friend, for a friendship that has already outlasted this degree and will outlast plenty more.

\vspace{1.2cm}
\begin{flushright}
\textit{Adem}
\end{flushright}
