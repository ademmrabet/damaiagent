\noindent I dedicate this work:

\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent\textbf{To my father,}\\
who taught me what quiet determination looks like long before I understood
the word. You believed in me on the days I could not manage it myself, and
every value I brought to this work started with something you showed me first.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{To my mother,}\\
for a love that never once wavered, for sacrifices whose size I only
understood years later, and for being the calm I could always come back to,
no matter how heavy this project got.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{To my brother,}\\
who has been a steady presence my whole life, and who never had to be asked.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{To my girlfriend,}\\
thank you does not cover it. You were patient on the nights this project took
more than I had planned to give, you believed in me when I doubted myself, and
you stood beside me through the hardest stretches.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{To my friends,}\\
Ayoub and Alaa, whom I met during this Master's and could not be more grateful
to have found. To Fayrouz and Houde, for always being there. And to Brahim,
my childhood friend.
