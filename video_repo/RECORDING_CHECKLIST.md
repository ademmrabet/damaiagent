# Recording checklist

Before
- [ ] Hosted app opened and awake, logged in, LLM mode Auto
- [ ] Browser at 100% zoom, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off (Windows Focus Assist)
- [ ] 1920x1080, microphone tested, quiet room
- [ ] Test questions pasted in a notes file so you copy rather than type

Recording
- Tools: Xbox Game Bar (Win+G) or OBS Studio, 30 fps
- Move the mouse slowly, pause 1 s after each answer appears
- Re-record any shot with a visible error rather than fixing it in editing

Editing
- CapCut or DaVinci Resolve (both free): cut, add lower thirds, voice-over from SCRIPT_5MIN.md
- Keep total at or under 5:00. Export 1080p MP4, H.264
- Burn in captions: the jury may watch without sound

Delivery
- Put the final in `final/`, upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Drive, paste the link in README.md
- Embed demo clips in slides 8 and 9: Insert > Video > This Device, set "Start: Automatically"
- Keep a backup copy of the clips on a USB stick

Safety
- No real credentials or personal data on screen
- Use a test account for the login and share shots
