# 5-minute showcase: narration script (about 650 spoken words)

Speak calmly, about 130 words per minute. Times are targets. Record voice-over after the screen capture, or live over it.

## 0:00-0:25 Hook (title card, then chat page)
"A new employee at the Bank asks: who must approve this mission program? The answer sits in a document of 79 pages. This is the DAM AI Agent. It answers in seconds, and it shows its source."

## 0:25-1:00 The problem (slide or landing page)
"The Delegation of Authority Matrix defines who initiates, approves, checks and is informed for every task. Searching it by hand is slow, and a generic chatbot would simply invent plausible roles. In governance, that is not acceptable."

## 1:00-1:50 Exact and natural-language questions (chat)
Type: `who approves 2.126`
"An id resolves with certainty. Notice the answer also lists who must check or verify, and who must be informed: those obligations are mandatory, so the agent always surfaces them."
Type: `who needs to be informed for quarterly mission program`
"Same task, found just from its name."

## 1:50-2:30 Resilience (chat)
Type: `who aproves the qaurterly mision program`
"Two typos, same correct answer. The correction is deterministic and works offline."
Type a French question, e.g. `qui approuve le programme de mission trimestriel`
"Ask in French, get French. The interface switches language too."

## 2:30-3:00 Honesty (chat)
Type: `what happens with 9.999.999`
"When the code does not exist, it says so. No guessing."

## 3:00-3:50 Platform (login, share, voice, upload)
"Each user has saved conversations. A conversation can be shared by QR code, with live notifications. You can speak your question, upload a document and ask about it."

## 3:50-4:20 Dashboard
"The dashboard shows the matrix at a glance: tasks per chapter and per action code, clickable to filter roles."

## 4:20-4:50 How it works, in one breath (architecture figure)
"The PDF is parsed once, character by character, validated, and stored as a knowledge graph of 327 nodes, 132 roles and 2,108 relations. The language model only rephrases retrieved facts, and a grounding check rejects anything that drifts."

## 4:50-5:00 Close
"Seconds instead of minutes. Sources instead of guesses. Thank you."
